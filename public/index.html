2. Description of Service

TunnelSnap provides automated, encrypted, time-limited reverse proxy tunneling allowing software engineers and QA teams to expose locally running development applications (such as localhost servers on ports 3000, 5173, 8080, etc.) to the public internet for remote mobile smoke testing, responsiveness verification, and client previews.

3. Micro-Passes & Billing

TunnelSnap operates on an on-demand micro-pass business model:

  • Every newly registered developer account receives 10 promotional Free Trial Passes of 5 minutes each.

TunnelSnap is strictly designated for legitimate software development, QA testing, and software demonstrations. Users may not use TunnelSnap to:

This Privacy Policy outlines how Pixorva collects, handles, and protects information gathered through TunnelSnap (tunnelsnap.pixorva.com). We are committed to transparency, minimal data collection, and developer privacy.

2. Information We Collect

3. Zero Packet Inspection & Zero Payload Storage

TunnelSnap acts as a blind, encrypted TCP and HTTP relay between your localhost machine and remote testing devices. We do not inspect, log, intercept, or store the body payloads, headers, cookies, API tokens, or data transmitted through your tunnel. Once packets reach their destination, they are never retained on our servers.
